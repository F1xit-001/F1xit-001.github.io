Idle Mining Tycoon

Pick & Profit

Tap to mine gold, dig deep, and strike it rich!

Grab your pickaxe and dig from rock bottom to a fortune. Pick & Profit is a laid-back idle mining tycoon where every tap breaks rock, banks gold, and gets you closer to riches — even while you’re away. Mine, upgrade, collect, repeat!

🎉 Surge events
Gold Rush, Diamond Vein, Lightning Pick, flying chests & more.
💤 Offline earnings
Your miners never clock out — collect while you’re away.
🌍 Play your way
Free to play, offline, one-handed, in 4 languages.

Dark-Fantasy Action RPG

Hollowbound

Take the road. Cut through what waits on it.

A knight walks a road that does not want him on it. Hollowbound is a portrait, one-handed side-scrolling RPG — run a delivery, fight what steps out of the dark, then come home and spend the spoils on better steel and stranger magic.

⚔️ One-handed combat
Time your strikes, guards and spells in portrait — built for a single thumb.
🔥 Ten upgrade trees
Branching paths for every spell and potion, each with its own trade-offs.
🛡️ Gear & set bonuses
Leather to Blacksteel — mix sets for two- and four-piece effects.
🐺 Bosses on the road
Goblins, bandits and worse, each with a pattern worth learning.
🏘️ A village to build
Blacksmith, magic, gear and quests between every delivery.
📴 Free & offline
No account required. Play it anywhere, any time.
